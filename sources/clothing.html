Clothing in Chinese: 76 Terms for Attire and Accessories

christina Date Published: 15 Apr 2013 Date Modified: 23 Feb 2025

Identifying different 衣服 (yī fu) or clothes in Chinese is essential (and common) in the day-to-day. Whether you’re shopping for a new pair of jeans or telling your friend they have a spaghetti stain on their tee, there are plenty of instances where you’ll be required to name various articles of clothing.

To round out your basic Chinese vocabulary, take a look at the 76 Chinese clothing words below.
